# Personas

## Riya - Returning customer
Wants to get back into her account quickly, usually on her phone. Not technical.
Gets frustrated by password lockouts and long support waits.

## Sam - Support agent
Handles tickets day to day. Wants fewer password-reset calls and a clear audit trail
for account changes.

## Dana - Support manager
Watches team performance and SLA breaches. Needs data she can trust and filter to her own team.
