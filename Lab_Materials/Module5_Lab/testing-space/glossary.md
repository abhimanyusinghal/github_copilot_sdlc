# Acme Customer Portal — Glossary
_(Same terms as Modules 2–3. Attach to Copilot Chat in VS Code.)_

- **Account** — a registered customer profile in the Acme portal.
- **Active user** — a customer who has logged in within the last **30 days**. (Canonical — use this.)
- **Ticket** — a support request raised by a customer.
- **Resolution time** — time from ticket creation to ticket closed.
- **SLA breach** — a ticket open longer than its target resolution time
  (P1: 4 hours, P2: 1 business day, P3: 3 business days).
  The lab fixture maps P2/P3 to 24/72 elapsed hours only; production calendar/timezone rules remain TBC.
- **CSAT** — customer satisfaction score (1–5) captured after ticket closure.
- **Self-service** — a task a customer completes without contacting support.
- **MFA** — multi-factor authentication.
